# Final Exam Info

The Final Exam will be held in-class on **Thursday August 1st** from **5:00 - 8:25PM**. You will have all of the normal lecture + lab time to work on the exam.

## Exam Policies

1. Note that the exams in this course will be open notes, open book, open internet, open Python interpreter, **open everything except ChatGPT**. I'm not stupid enough to think that I can stop you from using these during exams: all of you are probably more tech-savy than I am. So I'll just design the exams with this in mind 😊

2. The final exam will consist of several short-answer questions. You must submit your responses (one question response per page) using the Written Final Assignment on Canvas. You must submit your responses in **PDF** format. Read the **Submission** section below for more information.

3. You **are** allowed to ask me clarifying questions during the exam, but I will not always answer if the question is already answered in the directions (or would give part of the answer away). This is not to be rude, but to be fair to all students. If you ask a _truly excessive_ number of questions (to the point where it interferes with the administration of the exam), I will politely ask you to stop.

4. You **must** have your camera on during the exam so that I can proctor you. Virtual backgrounds are allowed. If you have a situation where you truly do not feel comfortable turning your camera on, please let me know and we can work something out.

5. There is a separate lab component to the exam: scroll down for more details.

## Conflicts and Accommodations

If you have a conflict with the exam time, you need to let me know **ASAP**. Technically attendance at every class session (including the final) is required: but I will do my best to sort things.

If you require an accommodation during the exam, please reach out to me directly via my Mt. SAC email.

The above instructions cover the written final exam. However, due to Mt. SAC requirements, I also need to administer a lab final. You can find the GitHub Classroom link to the lab portion of the final in the exam PDF above: make sure that you submit to GitHub Classroom (just as you have been doing for the other labs) **before the end of the exam time** and **before you leave the Zoom call** (I will be checking timestamps).

The lab portion of your final is just like the labs we've had so far, with some exceptions:

1. You can not have a partner.
2. You can not ask me for help.

Point differences between the lab and written portions of the final exam can already be found on Canvas. The two grades are combined into your overall final exam score, which is then weighted according to the syllabus.
